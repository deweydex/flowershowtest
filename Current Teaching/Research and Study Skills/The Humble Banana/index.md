(From Nov 10 (delay or release of brief due to lost voice on one of the days), research started October 20: In this class, we will be discussing framing, more importantly we will be discussing a highly contentious topic with broad and deep societal and historical significance: Bananas. This topic has too often been viewed as banal, mundane, quotidian, and other less fancy words for “everyday.” Together, we will approach this topic from four different frames, in an attempt to better understand not just the role of the humble banana in our society, but also how the way we frame facts changes their power, their relevance, and their scope. We will discuss argument, form, and tone, as well as investigating both what sources are easily accessible and what frames those resources contain within them. )
