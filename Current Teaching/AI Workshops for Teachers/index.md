title: "AI Workshops for Teachers"
---

These workshops are for teachers rather than students, in two strands. The non-technical strand is about what AI tools are, what they are not, and how to set expectations with students; the technical strand, From ML to AI, starts from fitting a line and ends with a working neural network, in notebooks that run on a school computer. Neither is assessed. The handouts and notebooks are linked from the pages below, and the code is in the [From ML to AI repository](https://github.com/deweydex/FromMLtoAI).
