What are we trying to write?
What help are we trying to get from the chatbot? What information do we need to give the chatbot to do its task?
 Tone: We would like the tone to be professional but with words that are not too full of jargon, or corporate sounding. In the end, we want this coverletter to be friendly and approachable.
 Format: We would like this draft to be at least 3 paragraphs, with a general introduction connecting our experience to the role, a main body describing some key aspects of our experience in more detail (and how those relate to the job description), and a conclusion that emphasizes our appreciation and excitement for the role.
 Context: What information would someone (or something, including an ai Chatbot) need to be able to complete this task?
I would need to give it a list of experiences (with job titles and descriptions) that I have had, as well as a few achievements or projects that I am proud of.
Job Description: either from the employer or summarized by you (perhaps you can even include a document with that information on it!) You could give the text of the advertisement (Links usually wont work on these things for reasons we will talk about, so lets think of it as copy-paste for now).
Why you would be a good fit for the job...

Address and specific contact details of employer (salutation, other technical parts of letter)
