# Resources for Learning Programming

- [Codecademy](https://www.codecademy.com): interactive lessons in the browser, good for a first week in a new language.
- [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org): short interactive problems in maths, logic and computer science.
- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org): free courses from arithmetic to calculus, and a good place to fill a gap you did not know you had.
- The Art of Programming
- [freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org): a free curriculum in web development, and a good set of articles on almost everything else.
- [Think Python](https://greenteapress.com/wp/think-python-3rd-edition/), Allen Downey: the book I recommend most often to a beginner who wants to understand rather than copy.
- [Python for Everybody](https://www.py4e.com/book), Charles Severance: free, gentle, and built around real data.
- [Invent with Python](https://inventwithpython.com), Al Sweigart: learn by building small games and automating boring things.
- [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/paths/foundations/courses/foundations): a full, free path into web development that asks you to build things.
- [MDN Web Docs](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development): the reference for HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a learning path for beginners.

# Resources for Learning Math(s)

- [Art of Problem Solving books](https://artofproblemsolving.com/store/list/all-products): the best written maths books I know for somebody who wants to think rather than calculate.
- [3Blue1Brown](https://www.3blue1brown.com): visual explanations of linear algebra, calculus and neural networks that change how the subject looks.
- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math): for practice, from the basics up.
- [Paul's Online Math Notes](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu): clear, free notes and worked problems from algebra through differential equations.
- [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org): see above; the maths side is where it is strongest.

# Resources for Literacy and Art

- [The Art Assignment](https://www.youtube.com/@theartassignment): a series that treats art as something you do, not only something you look at.
- [Crash Course Literature](https://thecrashcourse.com/topic/literature/): a fast, funny way into the books people say you should have read.
- *How to Read Literature*, Terry Eagleton

# Books on Education

## Modern Progressive Education

- *The Big Picture*, Dennis Littky
- *Leaving to Learn*, Elliot Washor
- *One Kid at a Time: Big Lessons from a Small School*, Eliot Levine
- *Radical Equations*, Robert P. Moses
- *Punished by Rewards*, Alfie Kohn
- *Creative Schools*, Sir Ken Robinson
- *Schule im Aufbruch*, Margret Rasfeld
- *Wir machen Schule*, Margret Rasfeld

## Theory-Oriented Books

- *Democracy and Education*, John Dewey
- *Experience and Education*, John Dewey
- *Pedagogy of the Oppressed*, Paulo Freire
- *Pedagogy of Hope*, Paulo Freire
- *Mindstorms: Children, Computers, and Powerful Ideas*, Seymour Papert

# YouTube Channels I Enjoy

- [3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown): mathematics, animated so that you can see the idea.
- [Crash Course](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse): a first pass over almost any subject.
- [The Art Assignment](https://www.youtube.com/@theartassignment): see above.
