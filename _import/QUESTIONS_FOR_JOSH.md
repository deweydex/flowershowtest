# Questions for Josh

Answers to these let the empty and stale pages be written from what you
already know, without guessing at your voice. Short answers are fine; a
sentence or two per question is plenty, and "drop it" is a valid answer.
Grouped by the page they feed.

## Home page

1. The bio now reads "I have since completed an MSc in Human Centred
   Artificial Intelligence at Technological University Dublin." Is the
   programme name spelt "Centred" or "Centered" on your parchment, and do
   you want the year (2026)?
2. Was there a dissertation or capstone project? A title and a sentence on
   what it found would give the bio, and a Current Projects page, something
   concrete.
3. The contact paragraph still asks for "timeline and budget" and mentions
   public schools and nonprofits. With the consultancy gone, is that still
   how you want work enquiries framed, or should it be a plain "what I am
   open to" line (workshops, curriculum, writing, collaboration)?

5. Do you want the DCFE address on the home page at all, or only on the
   Current Teaching pages where students already are?
