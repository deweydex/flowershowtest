The pages listed below are where my various teaching material and musings live. This will be an ever growing and changing set, which I will attempt to organize sensibly as I go.

If you are in one of my classes, start with [How My Classes Work](/Teaching%20Materials/How%20My%20Classes%20Work) and the [FAQ](/Teaching%20Materials/FAQs%20Frequently%20Asked%20Questions), then find your module under [Current Teaching](/Current%20Teaching). Some of the pages here are very much guides for the perplexed: opinionated writing designed to get you thinking and feeling about your learning, how you engage, and how you are best able to teach yourself. As you will hear from me in class, don’t be afraid to experiment, try things out, and write about what you did and what you learned. Your reflections on your learning adventures are just as valuable as what you learned!

If you are a teacher, an employer, or just curious, [How I Think About Teaching](/Teaching%20Materials/Guides/How%20I%20Think%20About%20Teaching) gives at least a snapshot of the goings-on inside my head, and [Materials and Earlier Work](/Teaching%20Materials/Assorted%20Teaching%20Materials) is the map of where everything I have made lives. Some of the material I have produced in the past is proprietary to businesses such as [Paper.co](https://paper.co) and Nelson Education, but I have tried to make available as much as possible, so that you can get a feel for how I think about the subjects I teach. All of my work is highly flexible in terms of medium, duration, learning outcome, and applicability to learning standards and competencies (I juggle at least four different national curricula when I conceptualize lessons). Most importantly, my material affords high levels of differentiation, individualized learning outcomes and adaptive assessments, as well as a variety of classroom set-ups and styles.

If you would like to use any of the resources I have produced in your classroom, or as part of a larger learning plan, and you are part of a public or non-profit institution, all I ask is that you let me know with an email and attribute my work as you would any other resource. If you are a private or for-profit institution, company or business, please send me an email and I am sure that we can come to mutually beneficial terms. In either case, I am of course happy to produce versions tailored to your teaching circumstances, with more restrictive copyright if that is what you are looking for. All roads lead to a quick message using the [contact form](/Contact), and I will get back to you as soon as possible!
