# Week of March 2

How to request an extension :)

Types of Extensions:

- Due to Illness, Death in the family, other emergency

- Due to unforeseen Work Conflict—this is very unlikely since due dates are often far away

- All the rest: usually a question of lack of preparedness

Words to think about:

Grace

Framing

Contrition (the sense of apologizing, or being apologetic)

Extenuating Circumstances, “Compassionate Consideration”:

- Illness: if there is a doctors note that specifies long term ailment (meaning longer than 3 days)

- Death in the Family: Usually 3 days, however, often the number of days relates to how “close” in blood relation you were to the person. EG: parent or sibling 5 days, grandparents 3 days, other stuff 1 day... note: friends often not included

- Emergency: Car crash, etc. dont bet on it—notify immediately

- Mental Health: look up forms especially for exams!

These can lead to extensions but are different things:

what are reasons you might need an extension that are not covered above:

procrastinating

forgot about the thing

vacation

overwhelmed—this is complicated (relates to mental health)

time mismanagement

laptop breaks—this is equivalent to dog eating homework

could not get given code to run
