# Development Process Worksheet

## For your GitHub Pages Site

**Instructions:** Answer these questions to create your “Development Process” page. Your combined responses should be approximately **400 words total**. You’ll then add this as a page to your GitHub Pages site.

---

## Section A: Planning & Approach (~150 words)

**1. What were your initial goals for this site?**

When I started this site, I wanted to…

**2. How did you manage your time while building this?**

To manage my time, I…

**3. What decisions did you make about what to include or leave out?**

I decided to include… because…

I chose not to include… because…

---

## Section B: Feedback & Iteration (~100 words)

**4. What feedback did you receive (from instructor, peers, or yourself)?**

The main feedback I received was…

**5. How did you respond to that feedback?**

Based on this feedback, I changed…

---

## Section C: Reflection & Growth (~150 words)

**6. How does this site support your career goals?**

This site helps my career by…

**7. What challenges or setbacks did you encounter? How did you handle them?**

The main challenge I faced was… I dealt with this by…

**8. What skills did you develop or strengthen through this process?**

Through building this site, I improved my…

**9. How will you maintain this site going forward?**

My plan for keeping this site updated is…

---

## Converting to a Site Page

Once you’ve completed your responses:

1. Create a new markdown file in your GitHub repository (e.g., `development-process.md`)

1. Add a title: `# Development Process` or `# About This Site`

1. Copy your answers (you can remove the question numbers and bold prompts if you want cleaner text)

1. Link to this page from your main navigation

1. *Optional:* If you want to keep this private after assessment, you can download the page and remove it from your public site

---

**Tips:**

- Be honest and specific - we want to see real learning, not perfection

- Use actual examples from your experience

- Keep it concise - don’t pad your answers unnecessarily

- Proofread before publishing
