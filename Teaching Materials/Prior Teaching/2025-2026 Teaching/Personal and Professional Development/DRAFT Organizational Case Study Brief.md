# DRAFT Assessment Brief: Organizational Case Study

## Personal and Professional Development 6N1949

**Weight:** 20% (Collection of Work)
**Deadline:** TBD

---

## Overview

You will analyse a mission-driven organisation, examining how it navigates real challenges around organisational change, conflict, delegation, budgets, and problem-solving. This is not a book report — you are expected to think critically about the organisation’s decisions.

---

## Choose One Organisation

Copy of Table 31

You will receive a case study worksheet with resources (budget documents, blog posts, press releases, news coverage) for your chosen organisation.

---

## What to Submit

A written analysis (600–1200 words) addressing:

### Part A: The Organisation (200–500 words)

1. **Mission and Structure** — What does this organisation do? How is it structured?

1. **Recent Challenge** — What significant change or challenge has it faced?

1. **Stakeholder Tensions** — Who has competing interests? (staff, volunteers, funders, users, public)

### Part B: Critical Analysis (200–500 words)

1. **Organisational Change (LO 6)** — How did the organisation handle change? What worked? What didn’t?

1. **Conflict and Resolution (LO 7)** — What conflicts emerged? How were they addressed (or not)?

1. **Resource Decisions (LO 8, 9)** — How do budget constraints shape what the organisation can do? How are responsibilities delegated?

1. **Problem-Solving (LO 4)** — What problem-solving approaches can you identify in their response?
