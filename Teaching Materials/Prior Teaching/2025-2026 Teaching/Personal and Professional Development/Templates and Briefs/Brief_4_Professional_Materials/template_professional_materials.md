## Component 1: CV Requirements

### Required Sections

Your CV must include the following sections:

- [ ] **Contact Information**

  - Name

  - Phone number

  - Email address (professional)

  - LinkedIn URL (if applicable)

  - Location (city/country)

- [ ] **Professional Summary or Objective** (2-3 sentences)

  - Who you are professionally

  - What you are looking for

  - What you offer

- [ ] **Education**

  - Institution names

  - Qualifications/degrees

  - Dates (month/year)

  - Relevant coursework (optional)

- [ ] **Experience**

  - Job titles or roles

  - Organizations

  - Dates (month/year)

  - 3-5 bullet points per role describing achievements

  - Quantify results where possible (numbers, percentages, outcomes)

- [ ] **Skills**

  - Technical skills relevant to the role

  - Software/tools you know

  - Languages spoken

  - Organized logically

- [ ] **Additional Sections** (choose what fits you)

  - Projects

  - Certifications

  - Volunteer work

  - Awards/achievements

  - Publications

  - Professional memberships

### Formatting Requirements

- [ ] Maximum 2 pages

- [ ] Consistent formatting throughout

- [ ] Professional font (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, 10-12pt)

- [ ] Clear section headers

- [ ] Appropriate use of bold, italics, bullets

- [ ] White space for readability

- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors

- [ ] Saved as PDF with filename: LastName_FirstName_CV.pdf

### Content Requirements

- [ ] Tailored to the specific role you are applying for

- [ ] Action verbs in experience descriptions (led, developed, managed, created)

- [ ] Quantified achievements where possible

- [ ] No personal information (age, marital status, photo unless required)

- [ ] Most recent experiences first (reverse chronological order)

- [ ] Consistent date formatting throughout

## Component 2: Cover Letter Requirements

### Structure

Your cover letter must follow this structure:

- [ ] **Header**

  - Your contact information

  - Date

  - Employer contact information

  - Professional greeting (Dear [Hiring Manager Name] or Dear Hiring Manager)

- [ ] **Opening Paragraph** (2-3 sentences)

  - State the position you are applying for

  - How you found the position

  - One sentence about why you are interested

- [ ] **Body Paragraph 1** (3-4 sentences)

  - Why this specific role interests you

  - What you know about the company/organization

  - How your goals align with their mission

- [ ] **Body Paragraph 2** (3-4 sentences)

  - What relevant experience you bring

  - Specific examples of your qualifications

  - How you meet key requirements from the job posting

- [ ] **Body Paragraph 3** (2-3 sentences)

  - What you will bring to the role

  - How you will contribute to the organization

  - Forward-looking statement

- [ ] **Closing Paragraph** (2-3 sentences)

  - Thank them for their consideration

  - Express interest in discussing further

  - Professional closing (Sincerely, Best regards)

  - Your typed name

### Formatting Requirements

- [ ] Maximum 1 page

- [ ] Professional business letter format

- [ ] Professional font matching CV (10-12pt)

- [ ] Proper spacing (single-spaced, blank line between paragraphs)

- [ ] Addressed to actual organization

- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors

- [ ] Saved as PDF with filename: LastName_FirstName_CoverLetter.pdf

### Content Requirements

- [ ] Answers: Why this role?

- [ ] Answers: Why this organization?

- [ ] Answers: Why you?

- [ ] Connects your experience to their needs

- [ ] Uses specific examples

- [ ] Demonstrates knowledge of the organization

- [ ] Professional but authentic tone

- [ ] No generic statements (could not apply to any job)

## Component 3: LinkedIn Profile Requirements

### Required Elements

Take a screenshot of your LinkedIn profile showing:

- [ ] **Profile Photo**

  - Professional photo or appropriate avatar

  - Appropriate for professional context

- [ ] **Headline** (must be visible in screenshot)

- [ ] **About Section** (must be visible in screenshot)

- [ ] **Education Section** (at least partially visible)

### Profile Content Requirements

- [ ] Professional tone throughout
