## About Signal

**Organization:** Signal Foundation (nonprofit)
**Mission:** “Protect free expression and enable secure global communication through open source privacy technology”
**Main Product:** Signal encrypted messaging app (no ads, no tracking, no data collection)

**The Challenge:** Signal must remain financially sustainable without advertising, data harvesting, or venture capital, while competing against well-funded tech giants.
