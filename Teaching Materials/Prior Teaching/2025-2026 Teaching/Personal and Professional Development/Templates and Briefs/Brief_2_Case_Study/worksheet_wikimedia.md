## About Wikimedia

**Organization:** Wikimedia Foundation
**Mission:** “Empower and engage people around the world to collect and develop educational content under a free license”
**Main Projects:** Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, Wikidata, and other free knowledge projects

**The Challenge:** Wikimedia must coordinate thousands of volunteer editors worldwide while addressing criticism about geographic and demographic representation in its content and community.

## Analysis Questions (800-1000 words total)

### 1. Understanding the Organization (150-200 words)

- What does Wikimedia do? How is it different from Wikipedia?

- How is Wikimedia organized? (Staff, volunteers, chapters, affiliates)

- What is Wikimedia’s funding model? Where does the money come from?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 3%, Skills Demo: 1%)**

---

### 2. Organizational Challenge (200-250 words)

- What challenges does Wikimedia face in global representation and diversity?

- How has Wikimedia tried to address these challenges?

- What tensions exist between volunteer editors and the Foundation staff?

- How would you approach improving diversity in a volunteer-driven organization?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 3. Decision-Making and Resources (200-250 words)

- How does Wikimedia decide how to spend its donations?

- What priorities compete for resources (technology, community support, legal, advocacy)?

- How does Wikimedia balance the needs of different language communities?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 4. Management and Structure (150-200 words)

- How does Wikimedia manage relationships between paid staff and volunteer contributors?

- What challenges arise in coordinating work across different time zones and cultures?

- What would you do differently if you were managing a global volunteer organization?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 2%)**

---

### 5. Personal Reflection (100-150 words)

- What surprised you about how Wikimedia operates?

- What appeals to you or concerns you about mission-driven volunteer work?

- How might you prepare for working in distributed, global organizations?

**Points available: 3% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 1%)**
