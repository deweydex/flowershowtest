## About Creative Commons

**Organization:** Creative Commons
**Mission:** “We help overcome legal obstacles to the sharing of knowledge and creativity”
**Main Work:** Creative Commons licenses, open access advocacy, global copyright reform

**The Challenge:** Creative Commons must adapt to AI-generated content, navigate different copyright laws globally, and maintain relevance as technology changes how we create and share.

## Analysis Questions (800-1000 words total)

### 1. Understanding the Organization (150-200 words)

- What does Creative Commons do? What are CC licenses?

- How is Creative Commons organized globally? (Network, affiliates, headquarters)

- How is Creative Commons funded?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 3%, Skills Demo: 1%)**

---

### 2. Organizational Challenge (200-250 words)

- How has AI-generated content challenged Creative Commons’ traditional approach?

- What questions arise when AI trains on CC-licensed content?

- How has Creative Commons responded to these new challenges?

- If you were leading Creative Commons, how would you address AI and copyright?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 3. Decision-Making and Resources (200-250 words)

- How does Creative Commons decide when to update licenses or create new ones?

- How does Creative Commons balance legal precision with user accessibility?

- What resource challenges does Creative Commons face as a small organization with global impact?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 4. Management and Structure (150-200 words)

- How does Creative Commons coordinate work across different countries and legal systems?

- What challenges arise in advocating for open culture in different cultural contexts?

- What did you learn about managing change in a standards-setting organization?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 2%)**

---

### 5. Personal Reflection (100-150 words)

- What surprised you about Creative Commons and open licensing?

- How does Creative Commons work relate to your field or career interests?

- What skills would be valuable for working in open access or copyright reform?

**Points available: 3% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 1%)**
