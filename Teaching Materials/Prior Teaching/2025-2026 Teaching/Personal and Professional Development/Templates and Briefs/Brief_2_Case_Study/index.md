# Assessment Brief 2: Organizational Case Study

**Weight:** 25% of final grade
**Submission Deadline:** [To be announced]

---

## Overview

Analyze a real organization facing challenges in change management, decision-making, resource allocation, and organizational structure. Choose from five mission-driven organizations and complete a structured analysis.

---

## Choose One Organization

Select ONE of these organizations:

- **Mozilla** - Restructuring and financial sustainability

- **Wikimedia Foundation** - Global knowledge equity initiatives

- **Signal** - Sustainability without surveillance capitalism

- **EFF (Electronic Frontier Foundation)** - Digital rights advocacy

- **Creative Commons** - Adapting open culture to AI era

You will receive a detailed worksheet for your chosen organization.

---

## What to Submit

A typed analysis (800-1000 words) completing the worksheet for your chosen organization. Your analysis must answer all five questions on the worksheet and use specific evidence from the organizational resources provided.

**Use the worksheet for your chosen organization:**

- `worksheet_mozilla.md`

- `worksheet_wikimedia.md`

- `worksheet_signal.md`

- `worksheet_eff.md`

- `worksheet_creative_commons.md`

**Use the provided template:** `template_case_study.md`

- [ ] Total word count is 800-1000 words

- [ ] Used specific examples from the organization

## Grading Criteria by Question

### Question 1: Understanding the Organization (4% total)

**Collection (3%):**

- **3 points:** Comprehensive, accurate explanation with specific details

- **2 points:** Clear explanation covering main points

- **1 point:** Basic explanation with some accuracy

- **0 points:** Inaccurate or incomplete

**Skills Demo (1%):**

- **1 point:** Shows understanding of organizational structure concepts

- **0 points:** No evidence of organizational thinking

### Question 2: Organizational Challenge (7% total)

**Collection (4%):**

- **4 points:** Detailed, sophisticated analysis with multiple perspectives

- **3 points:** Thorough analysis with good use of evidence

- **2 points:** Adequate analysis addressing the challenge

- **0-1 points:** Superficial or incomplete

**Skills Demo (3%):**

- **3 points:** Excellent application of change management concepts

- **2 points:** Good application of relevant concepts

- **1 point:** Some application of concepts

- **0 points:** No evidence of conceptual thinking

### Question 3: Decision-Making and Resources (7% total)

**Collection (4%):**

- **4 points:** Insightful analysis with strong use of evidence

- **3 points:** Clear analysis with good use of evidence

- **2 points:** Basic analysis with some evidence

- **0-1 points:** Superficial or incomplete

**Skills Demo (3%):**

- **3 points:** Strong strategic thinking and problem-solving

- **2 points:** Good strategic thinking

- **1 point:** Some strategic thinking

- **0 points:** No evidence of strategic thinking

### Question 4: Management and Structure (4% total)

**Collection (2%):**

- **2 points:** Clear understanding of management challenges

- **1 point:** Basic understanding

- **0 points:** Limited or no understanding

**Skills Demo (2%):**

- **2 points:** Shows sophisticated understanding of management complexity

- **1 point:** Shows basic understanding

- **0 points:** No evidence of management thinking

### Question 5: Personal Reflection (3% total)

**Collection (2%):**

- **2 points:** Thoughtful, specific personal insights

- **1 point:** Basic personal reflection

- **0 points:** Generic or missing

**Skills Demo (1%):**

- **1 point:** Clear connection to professional development

- **0 points:** No career connection
