## About Mozilla

**Organization:** Mozilla Foundation
**Mission:** “Ensure the Internet is a global public resource, open and accessible to all”
**Main Products:** Firefox browser, privacy tools, internet health initiatives

**The Challenge:** Mozilla relies heavily on revenue from Google for its search engine deal, creating tension between its mission of internet independence and its business model.

## Analysis Questions (800-1000 words total)

### 1. Understanding the Organization (150-200 words)

- What does Mozilla do? Who do they serve?

- How is Mozilla organized? (Look at their website - what teams or divisions do they have?)

- What is Mozilla’s primary source of funding, and why might this be problematic?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 3%, Skills Demo: 1%)**

---

### 2. Organizational Challenge (200-250 words)

- What major challenge or change has Mozilla faced recently?

- How did Mozilla leadership communicate this change?

- How did employees and the community respond?

- What would you have done differently if you were leading this change?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 3. Decision-Making and Resources (200-250 words)

- What difficult decisions has Mozilla had to make between its mission and financial sustainability?

- How does Mozilla allocate its budget and resources? (What do they spend money on?)

- If you were advising Mozilla, what would you recommend to improve their financial situation?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 4. Management and Structure (150-200 words)

- How does Mozilla balance different priorities (browser development, privacy advocacy, open source work)?

- What challenges does Mozilla face in managing teams during organizational change?

- What did you learn about managing in mission-driven organizations?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 2%)**

---

### 5. Personal Reflection (100-150 words)

- What surprised you about how Mozilla operates?

- How does this case study connect to your own career interests or development?

- What would you want to learn to work in a similar organization?

**Points available: 3% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 1%)**
