## Analysis Questions (800-1000 words total)

### 1. Understanding the Organization (150-200 words)

- What does EFF do? What types of issues do they work on?

- How is EFF organized? (Legal team, technologists, policy experts, etc.)

- How is EFF funded, and why does this matter for an advocacy organization?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 3%, Skills Demo: 1%)**

---

### 2. Organizational Challenge (200-250 words)

- What is a recent issue or campaign EFF has worked on?

- How did EFF decide to get involved in this issue?

- What challenges did EFF face in advocating for digital rights?

- How would you approach advocacy work in a politically divided environment?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 3. Decision-Making and Resources (200-250 words)

- How does EFF decide which issues to prioritize?

- How does EFF balance different strategies (litigation, policy advocacy, public education, technology)?

- What tradeoffs does EFF make when allocating resources?

**Points available: 7% (Collection: 4%, Skills Demo: 3%)**

---

### 4. Management and Structure (150-200 words)

- How does EFF manage work across different domains (legal, technical, policy)?

- What challenges arise in responding quickly to emerging digital rights issues?

- What would you do to manage an organization facing rapidly changing challenges?

**Points available: 4% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 2%)**

---

### 5. Personal Reflection (100-150 words)

- What surprised you about EFF’s work or approach?

- What did you learn about advocacy organizations and how they operate?

- How might skills in digital rights advocacy be valuable in your career?

**Points available: 3% (Collection: 2%, Skills Demo: 1%)**
