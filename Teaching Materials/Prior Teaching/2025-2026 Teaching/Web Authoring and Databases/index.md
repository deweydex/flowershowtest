-  Some questions you might ask:

  - How is the tutorial structured, what media are used? Is it a series of lessons? Projects? Videos?

  - What is your first impression of the tutorial site itself, before even beginning the tutorial? Is it flashy, minimal, fun/funny, professional... is it free or paid?

  - How long do you think it would take to do this tutorial? Does the site say how long? How deep/broad does this tutorial go?
