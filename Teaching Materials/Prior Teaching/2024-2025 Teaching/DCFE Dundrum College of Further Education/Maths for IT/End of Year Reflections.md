Hello fellow learners,

what a year it has been. For those of you expecting a standard feedback form, prepare to be surprised. As our school year comes to a close, there are few questions we might reflect on, these are emotional questions, not academic ones, but don’t let that e-word fool you: they are as much about understanding your subject as they are about understanding your learning.

Format: this is a webpage, so this isn’t really a form, per-se, the idea is to open up a document (whether word or .txt or a jupyter notebook if you, like me, have become addicted to markdown), and allow your answer to one question flow into the next. This means, unlike normal forms, the idea is that you read all the questions beforehand, and then choose those that most resonate with you. If you would like you can share the answer with me, if not, you are welcome to keep your answer to yourself. Obviously, I cannot force you to reflect, and there are so many pressing things that call for your attention these days, but I hope, given the level of trust that we have built over the past year as part of this class, that you will indulge me this more humanistic exercise. So, without further ado, take a breath, stretch, give yourself a moment to settle, and lets get reflecting.

1. Can you think of a particular topic or idea that you really struggled with before this year that now you feel you understand deeply? What brought about that transition?

1. What specific aspects of our class did you find helpful and enjoyable, what were the parts where you you changed your feelings or opinions? (For example, first you liked something but ended up find it less useful, or the reverse!)

1. What about the culture of the class worked for you or didn’t work for you? Is there some aspect that initially you were frustrated by but eventually you liked (or at least appreciated)? What parts of the cadence of the class worked, and what didn’t? How come?

1. How did you find the assignments and class time? Were you able to connect with me and with colleagues or did you prefer to work on your own? How did your impressions of the material covered in those assignments/classes change overtime?

1. How have your personal goals changed over the course of the year? Did something in our class change what you think and feel about your future?

1. What is a habit or pattern of behavior that you noticed this year that you would like to hold on to for next year (whatever that year might hold)? Is there a habit that you would like to abandon next year, or perhaps transform into something else?

1. How would you say your impression of our class’s subject has changed over the course of the year? What was surprising? What is something that if you told your past self, they wouldn’t believe it (or wouldn’t understand it!)?

1. When you think about your level of confidence in our subject, both overall and in class, what are the things that you would say increase that feeling of confidence? What gets in the way? Has this changed over the course of the year? If so, in what way? If not, is there some direction you would like to take to change your confidence level?

1. When studying a subject it can be easy to lose the big picture the “why am I doing this again?” feeling can be really strong: for your future self, what is something that you gained from this class that has changed the way you think about your field overall? Was there a time when you lost your perspective this year? What happened and what did you do to try to get yourself back on track? (Or, if you are in the middle of figuring that out, maybe think through some things you might do to refocus yourself and better understand your goals.)

1. Technical subjects can often feel separate from more creative subjects, but let’s try to abandon that bias! Can you think of a time this year that you felt really creative in our class? Can you think of a time where you were challenged and had to really think differently from the way you thought before in order to move forward? Were there moments where you started doing something a different way and that opened up your understanding?

1. How would you say your emotional state has changed over the course of the year (maybe think of a graph, are there lots of little ups and downs, some big swings up/down, is the curve smooth, or choppy...) ? What things outside of your education had a positive or negative effect on your studies?
