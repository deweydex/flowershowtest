# Primary and Alternative Learning Paths:

What are our primary and alternative learning paths?

Our primary learning path will involve a specific series of materials as well as practice problems to be learned and digested both in and out of class. Learning to program is a significant time commitment, and *learning how one learns* is an added, but essential, component to learning. In our classes we will have the structure outlined below, but this structure might not work for everyone. That is why we have an alternative learning path: this learning path is based more on individual goals and alternative resources: there will still be the responsibility of completing and submitting assignments, just as I will still be available to help with coding questions and any misunderstandings and conceptual struggles that may arise. There is no negative repercussion from engaging in alternative methods of learning in my class, however, if you find yourself struggling with your chosen method and resources, it is your responsibility to discuss concerns with me, and to seek out alternative means of learning the material we need to cover. With that in mind:
